SAFETY FIRST

4 Easy Rules to Identify Fake Schemes

payments

1. Advance Fee Demands

Demands for ₹200 to ₹2500 in processing fees, form checks, or tractor delivery charges are 100% fraud.

Never pay cash
pin

2. Asking for OTP or PIN

No government officer or scheme agent will ever call and ask for your bank OTP or UPI PIN.

Keep OTP secret
featured_seasonal_and_gifts

3. Unrealistic Free Promises

Messages claiming free brand-new cars, lottery jackpots, or ₹50,000 cash for filling a survey are fake.

False claims
link_off

4. Suspicious Website Links

Genuine government web portals ALWAYS end in .gov.in or .nic.in. Domains ending in .xyz, .top, .free, or .blogspot are fraudulent.

Check for .gov.in
