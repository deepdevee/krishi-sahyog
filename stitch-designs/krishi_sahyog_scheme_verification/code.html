payments
Advance Fee Demands पंजीकरण के नाम पर पैसे मांगना

Demands advance money, file charge, or "processing fee" for registration. Genuine government benefits never charge an upfront fee.

key
Asking for OTP & PIN ओटीपी या पिन मांगना

Asks you to share Aadhaar OTP, UPI PIN, or bank ATM PIN over phone or WhatsApp. No official officer will ever ask for OTP.

redeem
Unrealistic Promises असंभव मुफ्त उपहार

Promises unrealistic gifts like free brand-new cars, lottery jackpots, gold coins, or instant ₹50,000 cash for answering quizzes.

public_off
Suspicious Links नकली वेबसाइट लिंक

Uses strange website links ending in .xyz, .top, .free, or .tk instead of official government domains ending in .gov.in or .nic.in.
