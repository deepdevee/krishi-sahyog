PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi) is an income support scheme launched by the Central Government. It gives small, marginal, and landholding farmer families assured supplementary cash support so they don't have to borrow from local moneylenders at exorbitant interest rates right before sowing season.

spa

The fund empowers farmers to procure quality inputs such as high-yielding seeds, natural fertilizers, pest remedies, and seasonal tractor tilling services without waiting for delayed credit approvals.

Q: My PM-KISAN installment has stopped. What should I check first? (मेरी किस्त रुक गई है, क्या करें?)

Over 85% of halted installments happen because of two common reasons:

  • Pending e-KYC: Complete OTP-based e-KYC on pmkisan.gov.in using your mobile phone or fingerprint scan at any CSC center.
  • Bank Aadhaar Seeding: Visit your branch and ask for your savings account to be "DBT / NPCI Enabled". Simply having an account is not enough.
  • Land Record Re-verification: Ensure your land records were verified by your local Patwari during the state digitization drive.
Q: Can tenant farmers or sharecroppers apply? (क्या बटाईदार किसान आवेदन कर सकते हैं?)

Under current scheme operational guidelines, the agricultural land ownership document (Khatauni/7/12) must be legally registered in the applicant's name. Tenant cultivators without registered land title deeds are not covered directly under PM-KISAN, but can explore state-specific tenant welfare schemes like KALIA or Rythu Bharosa.

Q: Can both father and son receive benefits if land is partitioned?

Yes. If the adult son has legal land ownership documents registered separately in government revenue records in his individual name, he constitutes an independent beneficiary unit and is eligible to apply.
